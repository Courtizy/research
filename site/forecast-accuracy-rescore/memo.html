What was tested

The same companies, filing dates and first-reported actuals as Study 01, scored before and after the Valuation Model's changes. Before: the fading-growth forecast with a P10–P90 band from a ±3-point wobble in growth. After: a base case blending no change, the fade and the sector's growth, with ranges taken from the spread of past misses. Every forecast, sector median and range used only filings public on the day it was made.

Findings

    What this means in practice

      Limits

      • Mechanical forecasts from filings only. Analyst and management forecasts aren't scored.
      • Associations, not causes.
      • The blend's design was chosen after seeing this data. The ranges were scored out of sample; the base case's gain is not yet confirmed on forecasts made after the study.
      • Large US filers only (revenue of $1B or more in a cohort year), finance excluded.
