What this is

Every study so far scores forecasts against the past, so the method could have been tuned to it. This one can't be. The Valuation Model's engine and calibration tables were frozen on . Every week since, each new 10-K from a company in Study 01's or the held-out sample gets the model's default base case and its 80% and 90% revenue ranges for one to five years out, written to a log that can only grow: each entry carries the hash of the one before it. When a later 10-K reports the year, the forecast is scored.
