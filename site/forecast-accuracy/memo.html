What was tested

Four ways of forecasting revenue one to five years ahead were scored against what companies later reported: no change, each company's own three-year growth, its sector's growth, and the Valuation Model's fading-growth forecast with its P10–P90 range. Each forecast used only filings available on the day it was made.
